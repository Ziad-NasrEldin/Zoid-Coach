# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Zoid Coach initially serves Ziad, a macOS power user working across software development, design, media, client communication, research, and administration.

The primary workflow is daily planning and execution on a personal Mac.

The user needs to choose realistic priority work, begin it, notice drift early, recover without shame, and learn from actual behavior.

Future users may be independent knowledge workers who want private behavioral coaching without employer monitoring or mandatory cloud storage.

## Product Purpose

Zoid Coach is a standalone local-first macOS productivity coach.

It connects intended work from Apple Reminders with actual computer behavior from Screenwatch.

It uses macOS notifications for timely interventions and the Today dashboard for coaching choices.

The product closes the loop from intent through observation, intervention, review, and learning.

Success means the user starts priority work earlier, recovers from gaming or distraction sooner, improves estimates, and completes more meaningful outcomes.

The first release must remain useful with deterministic rules and no AI provider.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, perceptive, and firm.

The product should feel like a trusted private command room.

It is direct without becoming punitive.

It respects overrides and communicates uncertainty honestly.

Every recommendation should make the next responsible action easier to understand and begin.

## Anti-references

Zoid Coach must not look or behave like a generic SaaS analytics dashboard.

It must not use blue architecture, decorative gradients, glassmorphism, warm gold, neon accents, inflated focus scores, or novelty AI styling.

It must not rely on repeated identical cards or oversized decorative metrics.

It must not use shame, guilt, disappointment, or moral labels in coaching copy.

It must not hide missing telemetry behind false precision.

## Design Principles

### Action before analytics

The active task and next decision outrank historical charts.

### Smallest useful intervention

Observe first, then escalate only when evidence and policy justify interruption.

### Firm without shame

State facts, offer a concrete choice, and treat intentional overrides as valid decisions.

### Deterministic core

Critical state, time accounting, and intervention policy remain explainable and testable without AI.

### Local truth

Data-source health, coverage, and uncertainty are visible.

Sensitive behavioral data remains local by default.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

All primary workflows must support keyboard and VoiceOver operation.

Status must never rely on color alone.

The app must support reduced motion, clear focus order, large text, and sufficient contrast.

Notification actions must always have an accessible alternative in the main app.
