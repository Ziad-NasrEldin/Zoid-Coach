# Candidate claim

Scenario: `ZC-040-006`.

The user can explicitly skip a daily review and close that day without confirming any review conclusions.
Skipping must require a consequence-first confirmation and remain distinguishable from confirming the review.
Local observations, corrections, and notes must remain available after skipping.
The skipped state must survive restart, clear the unfinished-review prompt, and reopen safely after a later edit.
